Pattern · Field V
The V repeated at 6% ink on --bg. Loading, empty and onboarding backgrounds only. Never animated; never behind data.
CLEAR SPACE · One small-marble radius on every side of the mark or lockup (about 7% of the mark height); nothing enters it.
MINIMUM · Full Hybrid 64px. Simplified 33px. Below that, Drop v2 compact, down to 16px. Horizontal lockup 24px tall uses compact; stacked lockup needs 48px.
System

The Hybrid keeps what makes Field ownable, five marbles coming off the gate, and anchors it on one readable glass marble. When the field gets too small to read, the leader carries the brand alone as Drop v2 compact, and the Field V lives on as a quiet pattern.

WORDMARK · Barlow Condensed, Marble 600 and Rush 700 in --celebrate. The logo uses --celebrate, the same in both palettes, so the toggle never changes it.

LOGO REVEAL · 1.2 S · 0 to 0.6s the four small marbles drop in, staggered by 80ms from outer left to outer right, each with gravity easing (cubic-bezier .55,0,1,.45) and a trail that shortens as it slows. At 0.45s the glass marble falls through the middle of the V. At 0.6s it lands at the point with a 6% squash for 150ms and the “tok”. 0.75 to 1.0s the trails settle to their resting length and the V resolves into the Hybrid. 0.9 to 1.2s the wordmark slides in 8px from the left and fades up.
REDUCED MOTION · The static lockup appears with a 200ms opacity fade, with no fall, squash or slide. The sound cue still plays if audio is on.
RUNNING TIME · 43 seconds: hook 4s, product 28s, proof and tokens 7s, close 4s. The judging points land in order: impact (shared live race, 0:00), novelty (vertical procedural course and parimutuel pool, 0:04–0:28), UX (no SOL, one signature, proof in one tap, 0:12 and 0:32).
MUSIC · One track: a warm, low electronic pulse at 92 bpm, building at the drop (0:19), cutting to silence at lights out and for half a beat at the knockout, thinning under the proof, resolving on the logo. Voice-over is calm and close, not a hype announcer.
